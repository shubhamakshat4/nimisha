Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Cancellation Policy

We understand that unexpected schedule conflicts may arise. You may request a cancellation under the following terms:

  • Cancellation 24+ Hours Prior: If you request a cancellation at least 24 hours prior to your scheduled consultation slot, you are eligible for a 100% full refund.
  • Late Cancellation (Under 24 Hours): Cancellation requests made less than 24 hours before the scheduled session may attract a 20% administrative handling fee.
  • No-Show Policy: If a client fails to attend the scheduled virtual session without prior notice, the session fee will not be refunded. However, 1 free rescheduling opportunity will be extended.

2. Rescheduling Policy

You can request to reschedule your consultation slot at least 12 hours prior to the appointment time free of charge. Rescheduling is subject to slot availability on subsequent dates.

3. Refund Eligibility & Non-Refundable Scenarios

  • Completed Consultations: Once a 1-on-1 voice/video consultation session has been delivered and conducted, the service fee becomes non-refundable.
  • Technical Delays from Our End: If a session cannot take place due to an unexpected emergency or technical disruption on our side, you will be offered an immediate full refund or priority rescheduling.

4. Refund Processing Timeline

Once your refund request is approved:

  • Refunds are credited directly back to your original source payment method (Debit Card, Credit Card, Net Banking, or UPI).
  • The refund processing time typically takes 5 to 7 business days depending on your banking provider.
